Official Wildlife & Conservation Update

Maharashtra Mulls Leopard Sterilization for Population Control

To manage human-wildlife conflict, Maharashtra (ranking 3rd in India for leopard numbers) is considering scientific leopard sterilization and awaiting Central Government approval. Additional measures like habitat enhancement, scientific studies, and safety law reviews are actively being pursued to ensure peaceful coexistence between wildlife and neighboring local communities.

Explore Park Entrance Gates

Each gate offers a distinct ecosystem, unique tiger territories, scenic waterbodies, and safari routes.

Most Popular Entry Safari Track: 55 – 60 km

Karhandla Gate

Karhandla is the most famous gate of the Umred-Karhandla Jungle. Highly renowned for frequent sightings of **sloth bears**, Bengal tigers, Indian gaur (bison), nilgai, and spotted deer.

Resident Wildlife

Royal Bengal Tigers, Indian Leopards, Sloth Bears, Indian Gaur, Nilgai, Wild Dogs (Dhole)

Birdlife Highlights

Red-crested Pochard (migratory, Nov-Feb), White-eyed Buzzard, Crested Serpent Eagle, Scops Owl

Distance from Nagpur: 63 km (~1.5 hours)
Safari Duration: 4 hours
Insider Tip

Karhandla gate is closest to Nagpur and offers maximum luxury resorts nearby. Book early to secure morning slot permits!

Tigress Fairy's Kingdom Safari Track: 45 km

Gothangaon Gate

Famous for scenic beauty, multiple water bodies, and the **Indira Gandhi Backwaters (Gosikhurd)**. Gothangaon is situated 65 km from Nagpur and is easily accessible by personal vehicles.

Star Wildlife Attractions
  • Tigress Fairy (F2): Famous for her light-colored coat, celebrated for raising rare litters of 5 cubs in the sanctuary.
  • Surya Tiger: Dominant male tiger, son of famous Tigress Maya from Tadoba, ruling Gothangaon zone.
Migratory Birds

Red Star, Northern Shoveler, Northern Pintail, Red-crested Pochard (Nov – Feb)

Waterbodies

Gosikhurd Backwaters provide stunning wildlife sighting points

Zone Highlights

Quieter tracks and higher chances of waterbody sightings around Gosikhurd backwaters.

Riverside Scenic Zone Safari Track: 55 – 60 km

Pauni Gate (Pull / Khapri)

Perhaps the most picturesque entry to the forest, situated near the **Wainganga River**. Known for frequent sloth bear sightings, majestic tiger migrations, and rich bird diversity.

Iconic Resident Tigers
  • Tigress Shadow: Migrated 170 km from Moharli range (Tadoba) and thrives in this zone.
  • N4 Male Tiger: Major tourist attraction migrated from Ghodazari range, Nagbheed.
Distance from Nagpur: 90 km (~2 hours)
Key Avian Species: Oriental Honey Buzzard, Mottled Wood Owl, Indian Grey Hornbill
Wainganga River Front

Combine your jungle safari with local sightseeing near Gose Khurd Dam and riverside retreats.

How to Reach Umred Karhandla

By Air (Nagpur Airport) Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur is ~50-58 km away (1.2 to 1.5 hours drive).
By Train (Nagpur Junction) Nagpur Railway Station is the closest major junction. The park is a 1:30-hour direct road drive from the station.
By Road / Personal Vehicle Well-paved state highways connect Nagpur to Karhandla (63 km), Gothangaon (65 km), and Pauni (90 km).

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

The sanctuary is roughly 50–58 km from Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur, and about 1 to 1.5 hours' drive from Nagpur Railway Station.
Safari bookings are made through the official Maharashtra Forest Department portal (safaribooking.mahaforest.gov.in) by selecting your preferred gate, date, and slot in advance, as seats are limited.
The sanctuary is known for tigers (Fairy, Surya, Shadow, N4), leopards, sloth bears, wild dogs, gaur, nilgai, and over 180 species of birds, including exotic migratory species like the Red-crested Pochard across its dry deciduous teak forest.
Winter (November–February) for pleasant weather & migratory birdwatching, and Summer (March–May, especially April) for higher tiger sighting chances near waterholes. The park remains closed during the monsoon season.
There is no official Maharashtra Forest Department rest house or inspection bungalow located directly inside or managed specifically as a public stay within the sanctuary. Visitors can stay at private resort hotels located near the entry gates.
